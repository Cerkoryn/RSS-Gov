Instant checks
The rule is applied to today's real DReps, right here in your browser: who would be paid, how much, and how much someone gains by posing as several DReps. These numbers are exact.

      
Simulation
Your proposal opens as an issue on GitHub. Once a maintainer approves it, GitHub's servers run the programme 100 times under each of three reaction strengths, usually within a few minutes. The result is posted on the issue, with a preview of this site.

      
Comparison
The result is compared with every known design. Once merged, it is listed here and on the results page with its numbers, whether it adds a new trade-off or not.

      
Beyond this form
An idea the form cannot express, or a doubt about the method itself, is just as welcome: see How it works and CONTRIBUTING.md.
